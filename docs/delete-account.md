# Delete your noveltyOS account

**App:** noveltyOS
**Developer:** [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME]

## In the app (immediate)
1. Open noveltyOS.
2. On the Home screen, tap the **cloud icon** at the top, or go to **Settings → Cloud backup & sync**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Without the app
Email **[CONTACT EMAIL]** from the email address you signed up with, with the subject **"Delete my noveltyOS account"**.
We'll confirm your request and delete the account within 30 days.

## What gets deleted
- Your login (email address and password).
- Your membership in any business you joined.
- **If you own a business:** the business and all its cloud records, including products, stock history, sales, purchases,
  invoices, customers, suppliers, payments and settings. This applies for every member of that business.

## What is kept
- Nothing is kept in our database after deletion.
- Backup copies held by our hosting provider are overwritten as part of its normal backup cycle.
- Records stored only on a phone stay on that phone until the app is deleted from it, because we have no access to
  them. Signing out of Cloud Backup also clears them from the phone.
