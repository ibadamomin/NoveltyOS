noveltyOS ("the app") is an inventory, sales and invoicing app for small businesses. This policy explains what
information the app handles, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

## Summary
- Your business records are stored **on your phone**. Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on **Cloud Backup & Sync**.
- The camera is used **only to read barcodes and QR codes**. No photos or video are saved or uploaded.
- There is **no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data**.
- You can **delete your account and cloud data at any time**, in the app or by contacting us.

## Information the app handles

### Stored on your device
Everything you enter is saved in a database on your phone: products (names, barcodes, prices, costs, stock), sales and
purchase records, invoices and receipts, customer and supplier names and phone numbers, payments, and your business
settings (business name, address, phone, email, tax rate).

### Cloud Backup & Sync (optional)
If you create an account to back up your records or use noveltyOS on more than one phone, we collect:
- **Account information:** your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only in a securely hashed form by
  our authentication provider; we can't see them.
- **Your business records** listed above, so they can be backed up and synced to the phones in your business.

Cloud data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase (supabase.com), which processes it on our behalf. Data is
encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules ensure only members of a business can read or change that business's
records. Like most online services, the hosting provider may record technical information such as IP addresses in
server logs for security and reliability.

### Camera
The app asks for camera permission only to scan barcodes and QR codes. Images are processed on your device and are
never stored or sent anywhere.

### Sharing documents
When you choose to send an invoice, receipt or statement, the app creates a PDF and opens your phone's share menu. The
document goes only where you choose to send it (for example by text message or email).

## How we use information
Only to provide the app's features: storing, backing up and syncing your records, signing you in, and sending emails
you request, such as account confirmation or password-reset codes. We don't use your information for advertising or
profiling, and we don't sell or rent it.

## Sharing
We don't share your information with third parties except:
- service providers that run the app's infrastructure on our behalf (Supabase), under their data-protection terms;
- other members of your own business, when you invite them with your business's invite code;
- when required by law.

## Data retention and deletion
- Records on your phone stay there until you delete them, sign out of Cloud Backup (which clears them from the phone),
  or uninstall the app.
- Cloud data is kept while your account is active.
- **Delete your account:** in the app, open **Cloud Backup & Sync → Delete account**. This immediately and permanently
  deletes your login. If you own a business, it also deletes that business and all its cloud records. You can also
  request deletion without the app; see [Delete your noveltyOS account](delete-account.md).

## Children
noveltyOS is a business tool intended for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect information from children.

## Security
We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords and per-business access rules. No method of storage or transmission is
100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date. Significant changes will also be
announced in the app.
